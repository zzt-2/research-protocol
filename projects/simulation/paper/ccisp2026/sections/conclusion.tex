\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented an adaptive CPR algorithm for turbulent satellite--ground FSO downlinks. A fixed received-power rule selects and executes one DA or NDA carrier-recovery branch without turbulence-specific retuning. Fixed-algorithm sweeps show that their BER ordering changes with SNR, while stronger turbulence raises the high-SNR error floor. At 9~dB, adaptive CPR improves the common-payload BER ratio over fixed NDA by approximately 0.8--1.5~dB across the three regimes. The results demonstrate window-level adaptation between two CPR classes with a common output interface.
