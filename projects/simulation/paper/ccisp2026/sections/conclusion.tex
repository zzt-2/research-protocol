\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a two-layer DA/NDA estimator selector for carrier phase recovery in turbulent satellite--ground FSO links. A coefficient-of-variation gate identifies windows with pronounced power variation, after which a blind irradiance proxy and a fixed 13~dB effective-SNR level select between multi-pilot phase--time least squares and eighth-power NDA estimation. The design preserves the distinct strengths of the two estimators while keeping the online decision variables directly connected to received-window statistics. Across the prescribed AWGN and downlink evaluation grid, the selector recovers the lower-data-BER fixed estimator at 26 of 29 operating points. At representative same-SNR points, it reduces BER relative to fixed NDA by 2.3~dB in weak downlink turbulence, 2.0~dB in moderate downlink turbulence, and 1.3~dB in strong downlink turbulence when expressed as $10\log_{10}(P_{b,\mathrm{NDA}}/P_{b,\mathrm{SW}})$. Together with the observed DA--NDA crossovers and the NDA work-region advantages under stronger fading, these results establish a compact, interpretable path for matching the carrier phase estimator to changing atmospheric conditions.
