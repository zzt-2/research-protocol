\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented a two-stage DA/NDA estimator selector for carrier phase recovery in turbulent satellite--ground FSO links. A coefficient-of-variation gate identifies windows with pronounced power variation, after which a received-power-based irradiance proxy and a fixed 13~dB effective-SNR level select between multi-pilot phase--time least squares and eighth-power NDA estimation. The design uses received-window statistics available before carrier recovery and applies one fixed configuration across the evaluated turbulence regimes. In the predefined high-SNR work region, fixed NDA provides a total-energy-equivalent advantage of up to 3.1~dB over fixed DA, comprising a 1.9~dB data-symbol-SNR gap and the approximately 1.25~dB DA pilot-energy term. Together with the observed DA--NDA crossovers, these results support a compact and interpretable approach to matching the carrier phase estimator to changing atmospheric conditions.
