## Exact action / complexity contract

`input=QPSK CPE phase; state=single phase+quadrant offset; H=hard three classes, not parallel phase tracks; score=δ; merge/prune=N/A; lag=centered 2L+N−1 window; trigger=|δ|>threshold; fallback=no-op; reset=not defined; causal=buffered fixed-delay; output=corrected suffix/symbols; cost=O(1) rolling with O(N+L) memory; decoder-free=yes; single-tone=no.`

## M-C-A / collision / completeness

- M-C-A：VVPE+single-path PU/CS-DC 在 weak-turbulence coherent WOC 下假设 phase ambiguity detector 仍可靠；实际 noise/turbulence 可导致持久 slip。
- 四判据：1/2/4 PASS；近期 baseline 由专题 corpus 的 2021–2025 optical methods补足。
- D1=`CHEAP_NEAR`；D2=`STRONG_CHEAP_NEAR / ABSORPTION_RISK`。trigger 与 suffix repair 不是可独占 delta；仅 trigger 后有界 hypotheses+fixed-lag evidence commit 仍可区分。
- 实验完备性 V/V'/U=`2/3/1`。
