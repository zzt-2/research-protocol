- **Contribution**: N dual-polarization aperture inputs feed one complex 2N×2 adaptive FIR, jointly scaling amplitude, rotating phase, demultiplexing polarization, correcting linear distortion/skew and coherently combining. CMA for QPSK or RDE for higher QAM updates 4N complex FIRs without training (`content.md:43-51,61-97`).
- **Method overview**: I/Q correction and clock recovery precede the equalizer; FOC, carrier-phase recovery and demapping remain after it (`content.md:49-51,117-131`).

- **Baselines**: single-aperture/no-turbulence and theory are quantified; MRC/EGC/SC and block phase correction are discussed but not run end-to-end under the same conditions.
- **Conclusions**: 2/4 apertures improve Q² by 1.4/3.2 dB in moderate and 2.1/3.6 dB in strong turbulence. Skew 0/400/800 ps reaches the same floor but needs 12/20/28K symbols; beyond five samples convergence degrades sharply (`content.md:137-167,201-221`).

- **Fit**: direct PM multi-aperture combining baseline, with evidence limited to shared LO/clock, finite skew and mostly static/offline impairments.

## State

N/A as a learning state. Inputs are the N H/V complex streams and their FIR histories after I/Q and clock recovery.

## Action

Reward-free deterministic adaptation: CMA/RDE error triggers SGD updates of 4N complex FIRs, mapping 2N inputs to two coherently combined polarization outputs.

## Reward / objective

Reward=N/A. CMA/RDE blind objective minimizes modulus/radius error; downstream BER/Q² and convergence MSE evaluate the result.

## Model assumptions

Linear 2×2 per-aperture channels are coverable by finite FIR memory; LO and clock are shared; experiments use integer synchronization and ATT-emulated independent Gamma-Gamma irradiance (`content.md:53-83,123-131,175-201`).

## Network / algorithm

Non-neural 2N×2 butterfly FIR, 4N filters, CMA/RDE per-symbol SGD.

## Fit analysis

Signature: `{I/Q-corrected, clock-recovered N×H/V streams} -> blind 2N×2 CMA/RDE FIR updates -> polarization-demultiplexed, finite-skew-corrected coherent sum -> downstream FOC/CPR`. Dynamic outage tracking, confidence output and abstention are absent.
