# Reliability Analysis of MRC Diversity Reception System Based on LS and MMSE Channel Estimation

> Step 3 read: 2026-08-11 | title gate: PASS | extraction: `projects/thesis-fso/worker-logs/step-3-dsp-outage-read-l5-p0-boundary.md`

- **DOI**: `10.1109/ICCC56324.2022.10065885`
- **Source**: `papers/doi/10.1109_iccc56324.2022.10065885/content.md`
- **Read status**: fulltext read complete.
- **Publication status / venue / year**: formally published, IEEE ICCC, 2022.
- **Contribution**: The paper first analyzes LS/MMSE estimated-channel MRC under channel error, then proposes a genuine extra soft-weight action: per-antenna pilot attenuation produces pilot weights, linearly interpolated data-symbol coefficients `gamma`, which modify MRC (`content.md:29-81,141-177`).
- **Method overview**: comb-pilot LS/MMSE CE supplies `Hhat`; attenuation `alpha` independently supplies a continuous reliability factor, with deeper fades assigned smaller weights before decision.
- **Experiment setup**: MATLAB Monte Carlo, 1xM independent Rayleigh OFDM, 128 carriers, 32 pilots, 250 kb/s QPSK, linear interpolation (`content.md:179-194`).
- **Baselines**: standard LS-MRC and MMSE-MRC with varied antenna counts; no SC/GSC, hard drop, oracle CSI or alternative reliability weight.
- **Conclusions**: at 6 dB and M=4/8/16, relative BER reductions are 15/19/25% for LS and 12/17/21% for MMSE (`content.md:198-224`).
- **Relation to current topic**: occupies the broad pilot-derived soft-MRC action family; does not use coherent-FSO FS/CE/CPE validity, lock/outage state, hard drop, abstention or no-valid output.
- **Implementation details**: `alpha in R^(M x Np)` forms pilot weights; linear interpolation gives data-symbol `gamma`. Extraction did not preserve equations (15)-(16) well enough to assert their exact normalization (`content.md:161-177`).
- **Fit**: strong RF soft-weight neighbor and comparator precedent; not an exact post-DSP multi-source validity action.
- **Code**: no repository reported; MATLAB simulation only.
- **Validation**: local title/identity PASS; no external search in this step.

## State

N/A as a learning state. Observable quantities are received pilots, attenuation `alpha`, LS/MMSE `Hhat` and interpolated `gamma`.

## Action

Continuous deterministic action: deeper pilot attenuation produces a smaller weight; weights are interpolated to data symbols and applied to MRC. There is no zero/drop/abstention state.

## Reward / objective

Reward=N/A. Objective is lower BER without more antennas or a more complex estimator.

## Model assumptions

One transmit/M receive, independent noninterfering Rayleigh branches, AWGN, full diversity, comb pilots and linear interpolation (`content.md:29-31,151-183`).

## Network / algorithm

Network=N/A. `pilot attenuation alpha -> pilot weight -> interpolated gamma -> gamma-modified LS/MMSE MRC -> decision`.

## Fit analysis

The reliability input is attenuation, not LS residual/posterior covariance, synchronization margin, CPE coherence or decoder flag. LS/MMSE only supplies the underlying channel estimate.
